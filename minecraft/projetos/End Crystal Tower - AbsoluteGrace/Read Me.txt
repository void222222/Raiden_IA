Instructions:
Where to copy/paste

For .litematic > ".minecraft/schematics"

For .schem > ".minecraft/config/worldedit/schematics"

For .nbt > ".minecraft/saves/*WORLDNAME*/generated/minecraft/structures" 
(NOTE: If you don't see the "generated" folder, just add one. Then inside that add another folder "minecraft". And inside that add one more folder called "structures". Paste .nbt.)
